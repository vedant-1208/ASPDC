# Adani University — Campus Portal 🎓

A modern, responsive, and feature-rich campus web portal for **Adani University**. Built cleanly with vanilla **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🏛️ Campus Life & Academics**: Overview of academic programs, faculties, labs, and research centers.
- **🎉 Fest & Activities**: Interactive fest highlights, club activities, event calendars, and countdown timers.
- **🧭 Campus Navigation**: Interactive campus map & building guide to navigate departments, halls, food courts, and amenities.
- **🍱 Food & Dining**: Real-time cafeteria menu filters (breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner) with pricing, dietary tags, and stalls.
- **📊 CGPA & SGPA Calculator**: Complete semester-wise GPA calculator, percentage converter, and target CGPA planner.
- **📅 Class Schedule & Timetable**: Interactive day-wise timetable view for students across batches.
- **🌓 Theme & Accessibility**: Dark/Light mode toggle, smooth animations, and mobile-friendly responsive layout.
